PASS if the reply contains all of the following:
- at least one exit condition that names a metric, a numeric threshold, a period or date, and a concrete action such as rolling back prices;
- a forecast with a percentage of confidence and a check date;
- a block of disagreements where two named directors disagree about a specific point and the reply says what would settle it.

FAIL if exit conditions are vague (for example «пересмотрим, если не пойдёт»), if there is no forecast with a confidence and a date, or if the disagreements are generic.
